## Aggregators

One API for multiple Brazilian data sources.

- [BrasilAPI](https://go.mymarketing.click/apibr-brasilapi) - CEP, CNPJ, banks, holidays, DDD, NCM, rates and more in one open, community-driven API. `free` `API` `open source`
- [Invertexto API](https://go.mymarketing.click/apibr-invertexto-api) - Holidays, CEP, CNPJ, document validation and other utilities, with a free token. `free` `API`
- [CNPJ e CEP para agentes](https://go.mymarketing.click/apibr-cnpj-e-cep-para-agentes) - Remote MCP server for company lookup by CNPJ, CEP, holidays, DDD, banks, NCM, Selic, CDI and IPCA. `free` `by the author`

## CEP and address

Alphabetical order.

- [ApiCEP](https://go.mymarketing.click/apibr-apicep) - CEP lookup with a free plan. `free` `API`
- [OpenCEP](https://go.mymarketing.click/apibr-opencep) - Free and open CEP API. `free` `API`
- [ViaCEP](https://go.mymarketing.click/apibr-viacep) - Free CEP lookup in JSON and XML, no sign-up required. `free` `API`

## Companies and CNPJ

Alphabetical order.

- [CNPJ.ws](https://go.mymarketing.click/apibr-cnpj-ws) - CNPJ API with a free public version and paid plans. `free` `paid` `API`
- [CNPJá](https://go.mymarketing.click/apibr-cnpja) - CNPJ API with free public lookup and paid plans. `free` `paid` `API`
- [ReceitaWS](https://go.mymarketing.click/apibr-receitaws) - CNPJ lookup with a free plan limited per minute. `free` `paid` `API`

## Economy and exchange rates

Alphabetical order.

- [AwesomeAPI de cotações](https://go.mymarketing.click/apibr-awesomeapi-de-cotacoes) - Real-time currency exchange rates, free. `free` `API`
- [Dados abertos do Banco Central](https://go.mymarketing.click/apibr-dados-abertos-do-banco-central) - The BCB data portal, with series on interest rates, exchange rates, credit and Pix. `official` `free` `API`
- [PTAX no Olinda](https://go.mymarketing.click/apibr-ptax-no-olinda) - The official dollar exchange rate and other currencies, in OData. `official` `free` `API`
- [SGS do Banco Central](https://go.mymarketing.click/apibr-sgs-do-banco-central) - The Time Series Management System: Selic, IPCA, exchange rates and thousands of series. `official` `free` `API`
- [Tabela FIPE](https://go.mymarketing.click/apibr-tabela-fipe) - API for querying FIPE vehicle price table. `free` `API` `open source`

## Government and Legislature

Alphabetical order.

- [API do Portal da Transparência](https://go.mymarketing.click/apibr-api-do-portal-da-transparencia) - Federal government expenses, contracts, employees and benefits, with a free token. `official` `free` `API`
- [API Pública do DataJud](https://go.mymarketing.click/apibr-api-publica-do-datajud) - Metadata on court cases from all courts, from the CNJ. `official` `free` `API`
- [Catálogo de APIs do gov.br](https://go.mymarketing.click/apibr-catalogo-de-apis-do-gov-br) - The federal government API catalog at Conecta gov.br. `official` `API`
- [Dados Abertos da Câmara](https://go.mymarketing.click/apibr-dados-abertos-da-camara) - Deputies, bills, votes and expenses. `official` `free` `API`
- [Dados Abertos do Senado](https://go.mymarketing.click/apibr-dados-abertos-do-senado) - Senators, matters, votes and committees. `official` `free` `API`
- [Dados abertos do TSE](https://go.mymarketing.click/apibr-dados-abertos-do-tse) - Candidates, voters and election results. `official` `free`
- [IBGE: API de serviços de dados](https://go.mymarketing.click/apibr-ibge-api-de-servicos-de-dados) - Localities, names, aggregates, meshes and news from IBGE. `official` `free` `API`

## Open data portals

Full datasets to download or query.

- [Base dos Dados](https://go.mymarketing.click/apibr-base-dos-dados) - Brazilian public datasets processed and ready in BigQuery, with Python and R SDKs. `free` `open source`
- [Brasil.IO](https://go.mymarketing.click/apibr-brasil-io) - Brazilian public data in an accessible format, open source. `free` `open source`
- [Dados abertos da Anvisa](https://go.mymarketing.click/apibr-dados-abertos-da-anvisa) - Records of medicines, food and other regulated products. `official` `free`
- [dados.gov.br](https://go.mymarketing.click/apibr-dados-gov-br) - The Brazilian Open Data Portal. `official` `free`

## Tags

Each item says what it is. The tags next to it help you choose: official when it comes from whoever sets the standard, open source when the code is published, free or paid based on the usage model, API and SDK based on how you integrate. Items tagged "by the author" are made by Ciro Cesar Maciel and follow the same rules as all the others.
